### Week 3 Objectives:

- Use AWS CLI with a clear understanding of credential sources, Regions, output filtering, and errors.
- Explain the compatibility checks and asynchronous workflow involved in VM Import/Export.
- Separate schema conversion from data replication and identify migration validation and cutover requirements.
- Design scheduled EC2 start/stop automation around business hours, retries, permissions, and remaining costs.
- Interpret Grafana dashboards through their CloudWatch queries and authentication model.
- Use consistent tags and Resource Groups while distinguishing organization from access enforcement.

### Week 3 Achievements:

- **CLI reasoning:** Can distinguish identity, Region, permission, and output-selection issues before changing a command. This makes scripts easier to review and reduces the risk of operating on an unintended environment.
- **VM portability:** Recognized that an uploaded disk, an imported AMI, and a validated application are separate milestones. Compatibility and licensing checks must precede assumptions that a VM can be moved or exported.
- **Migration planning:** Can explain why converted schema does not prove data or application compatibility. A cutover plan needs validation, synchronization checks, and a defined fallback rather than only a successful migration task status.
- **Cost automation:** Connected business schedules to cron semantics, state-aware retries, and least-privilege roles. Savings depend on actual stopped hours and remaining resources, while notifications need to distinguish requests from completed transitions.
- **Dashboard quality:** Can trace a chart back to its data source and query settings. A useful visualization requires correct measurements, suitable statistics, and a secure authentication method before its appearance matters.
- **Resource governance:** Understood tags as a shared convention that drives grouping and automation. Their usefulness depends on consistent values and controlled modification, especially when tags also influence access decisions.
