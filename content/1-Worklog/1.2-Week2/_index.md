### Week 2 Objectives:

- Understand AWS storage, S3, CloudFront, and backup/hybrid storage concepts.
- Learn RDS availability, read scaling, security, and recovery.
- Study Lightsail, EFS/FSx, and the role of MGN in server migration.
- Understand EC2 Auto Scaling and Elastic Load Balancing.
- Learn CloudWatch metrics, logs, alarms, and dashboards.
- Understand hybrid DNS with Route 53 Resolver.

### Week 2 Achievements:

- **Storage:** Understood S3, CloudFront, archival, backup, and hybrid storage concepts.
- **RDS:** Distinguished Multi-AZ from Read Replicas and understood backup/recovery options.
- **Lightsail & related services:** Understood Lightsail, EFS/FSx, and MGN use cases.
- **Auto Scaling & ELB:** Understood scaling policies, health checks, and load distribution.
- **CloudWatch:** Understood metrics, log analysis, alarms, SNS notifications, and dashboards.
- **Hybrid DNS:** Understood Resolver endpoints, rules, and bidirectional DNS resolution.
