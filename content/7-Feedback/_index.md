### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is friendly and open. Members of the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) team are always ready to help when I get stuck, even outside working hours, so I never felt alone with a difficult problem. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps me stay focused. One thing I would love to see is more social gatherings or team bonding activities, so interns from different groups can get to know each other better.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors give detailed guidance, explain patiently when I do not understand, and always encourage me to ask questions. What I value most is that they let me try and solve problems on my own first, then step in with hints instead of handing over the answer. The admin team handles paperwork, provides the documents I need, and keeps everything running smoothly so I can focus on learning.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The work fits well with what I studied at university, such as programming, databases, and software architecture. At the same time, it introduced me to areas I had never touched before, including cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, and generative AI on AWS. Building the Bite to Business project helped me connect theory with a real system that runs in production.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I gained hands-on experience with many AWS services, from networking and containers to serverless and Amazon Bedrock. Beyond technical skills, I improved my teamwork, planning, and technical writing through weekly reports, blog posts, and the workshop. The mentors also shared real-world stories and career advice that helped me see more clearly which direction I want to grow in.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture is positive: people respect each other, work seriously, and still keep things fun. When deadlines get tight, everyone pitches in and supports one another regardless of role. Attending AWS events and community sessions also made me feel part of a larger cloud community, not just an intern working on a task list.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program offers flexible working hours when needed, which made it easier to balance the internship with my studies. Access to learning resources, internal training sessions, and AWS events is a big plus that I would not easily get elsewhere.

### Additional Questions

- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?
  - Seeing our team's project go from an idea to a website running on AWS, and the enthusiasm of the mentors who supported us along the way. The flexible schedule also helped me learn at my own pace.

- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?
  - Organize more hands-on study sessions and events, especially in the middle of the program when teams start building their projects.

- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?
  - Yes. AWS is used all over the world, so learning it in a real project with experienced mentors is a great foundation for a cloud or software career. The skills and connections from this program open many job opportunities.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?
  - It would help to have short checkpoint sessions where mentors review each team's architecture early, before teams spend too much time in one direction.

- Would you like to continue this program in the future?
  - Yes, I would love to continue, either as a participant in advanced programs or by helping future interns.

- Any other comments (free sharing):
  - Thank you to the mentors and the FCJ team for a meaningful internship. I learned not only about AWS but also about how to work, learn, and grow in a professional environment.
